Privacy Policy

LiteConnect · Last updated 9 September 2026

LiteConnect does not have user accounts, does not run analytics, and does not collect anything about you by default. This document says exactly what leaves your phone, when, and to whom — including the parts that are unavoidable in any wallet that talks to a blockchain.

The short version. Your recovery phrase and private keys are generated on your device and never leave it in readable form. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising and no tracking of any kind in this app. Two things do leave your phone: your public addresses, which any wallet must send to a blockchain server to learn your balance, and — only if you choose it — an encrypted backup that nobody but you can open.

1. What we never collect

  • Your recovery phrase, private keys or PIN. These are generated on the device and stored in Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences under a master key held in the Android Keystore. They are never transmitted in readable form, to us or to anyone.
  • Your biometric data. Fingerprint and face authentication are handled entirely by Android; the app is only told whether authentication succeeded.
  • Analytics, usage statistics, device identifiers, advertising IDs or crash reports. No such library is present in the application.
  • Your name, phone number, address or contacts.

2. What necessarily leaves your device

Blockchain queries

To show your balance and transaction history, the app sends your public addresses to Litecoin blockchain servers (Blockbook instances and servers speaking the Electrum protocol). This is how every non-custodial wallet works: a balance cannot be known without asking the network about the addresses that hold it.

Those servers are operated by third parties, not by us. They can see the addresses you ask about and the IP address you ask from, and could in principle associate them. We do not run these servers and do not receive that data. If this matters to you, route the app through a VPN or Tor.

Market prices

The app requests the Litecoin price from public market-data APIs (Binance, CoinGecko, CoinPaprika). These requests contain no information about you, your wallet or your balance — only a request for a public price. Your IP address is visible to those services, as it is to any website you open.

3. Optional features you have to turn on

Google sign-in and encrypted cloud backup

The wallet works fully without this. If you choose it, the following is stored under your Google account identifier in Google Firebase:

StoredFormWhy
Recovery phrase Encrypted — Argon2id key derivation, AES-256-GCM, using a password you choose So you can restore your wallet on a new device
One receiving address Plain text So the app can show you which wallet is about to be restored, before you type your password
Address type and network Plain text To restore the wallet with the same settings
Email address and account ID Provided by Google Sign-In To identify which backup is yours

The encryption happens on your device before anything is uploaded. The password is never transmitted and is not recoverable — not by us, not by Google, not by anyone. If you lose it, that backup can never be opened again.

What a stolen Google account would expose. Someone with access to your Google account could see the single receiving address stored above, and could therefore look up that address's balance and history on any public block explorer. They could not decrypt your recovery phrase or spend your coins without your backup password. We deliberately do not store your account's extended public key, which would have exposed every address you ever use rather than one.

You can delete this backup at any time from within the app, which removes the record entirely.

Camera

The camera permission is used for one purpose: scanning QR codes containing Litecoin addresses. Images are processed on the device in real time and are never stored, uploaded or retained. The permission is requested only when you open the scanner, and the wallet works without granting it.

Notifications

Used only for local alerts about your own wallet activity. No push service receives your data.

4. Children

LiteConnect is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Your control

  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data, including your encrypted keys. Make sure you have written down your recovery phrase first — without it, uninstalling means the coins are unreachable forever.
  • Deleting your cloud backup from within the app removes the stored record.
  • Because the wallet is non-custodial, there is no account for us to close and no balance for us to return. We never had it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is published here with a new date, and the change is recorded in the public commit history of the website's repository, so any edit can be seen and dated by anyone.

7. Verifying all of this

You do not have to take our word for any of the above. LiteConnect is open source under the MIT licence: every network request, every stored field and every use of the camera can be read in the source at github.com/litecoinconnect/liteconnect.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does, can be raised at github.com/litecoinconnect/liteconnect/issues.
